    How is this calculated?

    Formula: Mahana andaza = (Rozana views × 30 ÷ 1,000) × RPM × Niche asar

    RPM ka matlab hai creator ko 1,000 views par asal mein kitne dollar milte hain. Ye woh raqam nahi jo advertiser deta hai (use CPM kehte hain) — RPM us ke baad ki, creator ke hisay wali raqam hai.

    • Har view par paisa nahi banta. Sirf un views par kamai hoti hai jin par asal mein ad chala (monetized views). Isi liye ye calculator jaan boojh kar darmiyani, mehfooz range istemal karta hai.
    • Platform aur mulk sab se bara farq hai. USA/UK ki audience par advertiser zyada deta hai, Pakistan ki audience par bohat kam — is liye wohi views aik mulk mein zyada aur doosray mein kam kamate hain.
    • Niche ka asar (rough estimate): Niche multipliers sirf rough estimate hain, pakki raqam nahi. Tech jaisay mauzu par advertiser zyada deta hai, comedy par kam lekin views zyada aate hain — calculator is farq ko aik chhota sa zarb laga kar dikhata hai.
    • Niche multipliers zyada tar YouTube Studio data (300 channels) se nikale gaye hain, aur Facebook/TikTok par farq mukhtalif ho sakta hai. Education jaise niches ke andar bhi channels ka farq bohat bada hota hai.
    • Short video / Reel: Facebook Reels par RPM $0.02–$0.20 aur YouTube Shorts par $0.03–$0.08 rakha gaya hai, is liye short format ka andaza long video se bohat kam aata hai. Short RPM par mulk ka zarb bhi lagta hai (rough estimate): USA 1.0, UK 0.9, UAE 0.5, Pakistan 0.15 — ye bhi rough estimate hai, pakki raqam nahi.
    • Misaal: 50,000 views rozana × 30 = 15 lakh views mahana. Agar RPM $3 hai to 1,500 × $3 = takreeban $4,500 mahana — phir niche ka rough asar lagta hai.
